# Business rules (Level 1)

These are the exact rules implemented in `web/src/domain/*` (TypeScript) and
`web/supabase/migrations/20261010000300_functions.sql` (Postgres). The database
is authoritative for every money movement; the TypeScript copy powers what the
UI shows. The two are kept identical by shared test vectors
(`web/src/domain/pricing.fixtures.json`), which are asserted against both.

## Money

- All amounts are **integer paise** (`bigint` in Postgres; ₹1 = 100 paise). No
  floating-point value ever represents money.
- Percentages are basis points (1% = 100 bps).
- Rounding is **half-up integer division**: `floor((2n + d) / (2d))`. This
  deliberately differs from the prototype's Python `round()`, which rounds
  half-to-even. Example: ₹2.50 rounds to ₹3, not ₹2.
- Every amount is **simulated sandbox money**. `ledger_entries.is_sandbox` is
  constrained to `true`.

## Dutch auction

Given start price `S`, floor `F`, listing time `L` and departure `D`:

1. The decay window is `[L, D − 2h]`, measured in whole seconds (floored).
2. Price falls **linearly** (the prototype's α = 1): `raw = S − (S − F) × elapsed / span`.
3. `raw` is rounded half-up to the nearest **₹5**, then **clamped to `[F, S]`**.
   The clamp fixes a prototype bug where rounding a floor such as ₹152 gave ₹150.
4. Before `L` the price is `S`. At or after `D − 2h` it is `F`.
5. A listing with **no departure** ("keeper") never decays.
6. If `D − 2h ≤ L` (an empty window), the price is `F`.
7. The price is **fixed at claim time by the database** (`rio_current_price(..., now())`).
   The price shown in the UI is labelled "as of HH:MM".
8. Changing the start price, floor or departure **restarts the auction**
   (`listed_at := now()`), so a seller can't skip down the curve.

| Phase | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| decaying | `t < D − 2h` | price falling |
| floor | `D − 2h ≤ t < D − 1h` | at floor, still claimable and rentable |
| final hour | `D − 1h ≤ t < D` | **no claims or rentals**; a warden may route it to staff |
| departed | `t ≥ D` | still routable to staff |

A new listing's departure must be at least 3 hours ahead and at most 366 days ahead.

## Claims (purchases)

- Only `student` accounts can claim. You can't claim your own listing.
- The listing row is locked (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE`), so concurrent claims are
  serialized. A partial unique index allows at most one open claim
  (`PENDING_HANDOVER` or `DISPUTED`) per listing. Cancelled claims don't block re-claiming.
- **Anti-hoarding:** one open or completed purchase per category per student
  (the "Other" category is exempt).
- On claim, the price is moved from the buyer's wallet into escrow (`CLAIM_HOLD`).
  The seller is paid (`SALE_PAYOUT`) only when the buyer scans the seller's
  handover code. Either party may cancel before handover for a full refund (`CLAIM_REFUND`).

## Rentals

| Period | Rent | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Day | 2% of start price | 24 h |
| Week | 10% | 168 h |
| Month | 25% | 720 h |

- Deposit: **20% of the start price**. Rent and deposit are each rounded half-up to whole rupees.
- Eligibility: the listing must be rentable and in its trading window, and the time until
  departure must be at least the duration plus 24 hours. Keeper listings are always eligible.
- At booking, rent and deposit are both held. At **pickup** (renter scans the owner's code)
  the rent goes to the owner and `due_at` is set. At **return** (owner scans the renter's
  code) the full deposit is refunded. Before pickup, either party may cancel for a full refund.
- One open rental per listing (partial unique index).

## Rent-to-own

- Credit = **50% of the rent paid** on the rental, rounded half-up to the paisa.
- Buy price = `max(floor, current auction price − credit)`. Unused credit is not paid out.
- The deposit is refunded in the same transaction, before the purchase is charged.
- Allowed while the rental is `ACTIVE`, including in the owner's final hour.

## Damage and disputes

- The proposed damage deduction is **25% of the deposit**, rounded half-up to the rupee.
  The owner gets the deduction and the renter gets the rest.
- A deduction can only come from a **warden decision**. Owners can report damage when they
  scan a return code, but that opens a dispute and freezes the deposit. It doesn't deduct anything.
- Only a warden **assigned to the listing's hostel** (or an admin) may decide, and never
  someone who is a party to the dispute.
- Outcomes:
  - **Purchase:** refund the buyer, or release the payment to the seller.
  - **Rental before pickup:** cancel and refund rent and deposit, or dismiss (back to pickup).
  - **Rental after pickup:** full deposit refund, a 25% damage deduction, or dismiss.
    Dismiss isn't available once the item has been returned.

## Handover codes

- Generated by the database: 10 random bytes (80 bits), encoded as a 16-character
  Crockford base32 code. Only its SHA-256 hash is stored. The plaintext is returned once.
- They expire after **10 minutes**, work **once** (the atomic `UPDATE … WHERE consumed_at IS NULL`),
  and are replaced when a newer code is issued for the same handover.
- **The giver shows the code; the receiver scans it.** The receiver must be the expected
  counterparty. For staff custody, the receiver is any staff member or warden assigned to the
  listing's hostel, other than the owner.

## Staff transfers (final hour)

- A scoped warden may route an `ACTIVE` or `WITHDRAWN` listing to staff only when
  `now ≥ departure − 1h`. Each listing can be routed once (`UNIQUE(listing_id)`).
- The owner shows a custody code. Staff or a warden scans it to accept custody,
  and the listing becomes `STAFF_CUSTODY`. Custody can't be accepted twice.

## Sustainability estimates

These are coefficients from the prototype (`rio/engine.py`). They are **estimates,
not measurements**:

| Category | Embodied CO₂e | Tare | Volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Air cooler | 62.0 kg | 14.5 kg | 0.28 m³ |
| Foam mattress | 44.0 kg | 11.2 kg | 0.35 m³ |
| Kettle / immersion rod | 15.5 kg | 1.2 kg | 0.01 m³ |
| Drafter + board | 8.2 kg | 1.8 kg | 0.04 m³ |
| Study table + chair | 52.0 kg | 18.0 kg | 0.42 m³ |
| Other | no estimate | — | — |

- Sale or staff transfer: embodied CO₂e + 2.2 kg of avoided logistics (8 km × 0.275 kg/km).
- Completed rental: embodied CO₂e × 0.5 (substitution rate).
