# RIO Levels Roadmap

## Level 1 — Foundation Build (Current Focus)
**Goal:** A completely redesigned, persistent, authenticated, accessible, tested and pilot-ready application.
- Next.js + Supabase architecture.
- Real authentication and profiles.
- Persistent database for items, claims, and rentals.
- Functional QR handover workflow and warden dashboard.
- Explicitly simulated payments and sandbox transactions.

## Level 2 — The Pilot (Future)
**Goal:** Real payment collection, monitored operations.
- Actual payment gateway integration (Stripe/Razorpay) for deposits, rentals, and purchases.
- Webhooks and email/SMS notifications for expiring claims and handovers.
- Official university student verification (e.g., `.edu` email domains or SSO).
- Integration with campus administration tools for real "No-Dues" clearance.

## Level 3 — Scale & Expand (Long-Term)
**Goal:** Multi-campus operations, advanced analytics.
- Multi-tenant architecture supporting thousands of campuses.
- Open APIs for external client interfaces.
- Advanced abuse prevention (AI moderation for listings).
- Measurement and verification of real environmental outcomes (integrating with campus waste management data).
